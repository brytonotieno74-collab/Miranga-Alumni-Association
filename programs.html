Postgraduate Programs

M.Sc. /Ph.D in Agronomy & Crop Protection

Focuses on ptimizing crop yield, field management, seed technology, weed science, and crop Protection strategies.

Entry Requirements

B.Sc. in Agriculture, Horticulture, Crop Science, or related plant sciences with Upper Second Class Honours (or Lower Second Class with 2+ years experience).

Apply Today

M.Sc./Ph.D. in Agricultural Economics

Combines economics theory, quantitative analysis, and policy formulation to manage agricultural resources, commodity value chains, farm risk, and agribusiness trade. It equip scholar to evaluate agricultural markets, rural development, and food security policy.

Entry Requirements

B.Sc. in Agricultural Economics, Agribusiness Management, Economics, or related fields with Upper Second Class Honours (or Lower Second Class with 2+ years experience).

Apply Today

M.Sc./Ph.D. in Soil Science & Nutrient Management

Examines soil chemistry, physics, biology, land classification, and plant nutrient management. It trains researchers to tackle soil degradation, improve fertilizer efficiency, restore degraded land, and manage sustainable soil-water-crop ecosystems.

Entry Requirements

M.Sc. in Agriculture, Soil Science, Environmental Science, or related agricultural physical Sciences with Upper Second Class Honours (or Lower Second Class with 2+ years experience).

Apply Today

M.Sc./Ph.D in Animal Breeding & Nutrition

Deals with genetic improvement of livestock, molecular genetics, animal feed processing, digestive physiology, and precision nutrition. It prepares specialists to enhance livestock productivity, meat/milk yields, and climate-resilient animal husbandry practices.

Entry Requirements

M.Sc. in Animal Science, Veteriary Medicine, Agriculture, or related livestock sciences with Upper Second Class Honours (or Lower Second Class with 2+ years experience).
