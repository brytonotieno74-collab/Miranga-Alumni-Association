MIRANGA ALUMNI ASSOCIATION

HISTORY

The history of the website is fully dependent on the group that was formed by the 2019 cohorts.The group began by few individuals who saw it wise to embrace unity and brotherhood that was once defined in common physical environment during the primary study era.It was and it has been evident that the number of members has progressed and almost attaining the maximum required number for particularly the 2019 cohorts. This progession has been attained by members who tirelessly looked for the other fellows and added them to the unifying group. The members have been embracing unity, speaking a common language and offering each other a shoulder to lean on even in times of difficulties. They have shared project ideas that have been implemented, and have become evidently significant to individual members and a group as a whole. Members are looking forward not just for their reunion but also other cohorts to build up a solid structure of the school which once instilled in them basic knowledge and life skills. This movement has more to achieve!

MISSION

To be vibrant, lifelong virtual home that unites and empowers all former students and staff of Miranga, fostering dynamic social connections and mutual support regardless of the distance

VISION

To build an ever-growing community where past, present, and future Miranga cohorts remain connected, supported, and united beyond graduation.
